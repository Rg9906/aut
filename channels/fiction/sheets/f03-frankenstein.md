# Recording sheet: Frankenstein was started by an eighteen year old

File name: **f03-frankenstein.m4a** (or .wav / .mp3). Target length about 40 seconds. Mode: **dual**.

## How to record
- Phone voice memo, quiet room, phone about a hand-width from your mouth. No music or fan.
- One take, start to finish. If you flub a line, pause, say it again, keep going (I keep the matching words).
- Leave about one second of silence between numbered lines. Read the words exactly as written.
- Say it with energy. It should feel like a chat with a friend, a bit more hyped than normal.

- Two characters, both you: **PANEL** = the hyped one, **PLOT** = the calm, smug one. Change your tone a little for each.

## Read this

1. [PANEL] Frankenstein started as a dare! And the writer was only eighteen!
   _(tone: surprised, big energy)_

2. [PLOT] A rainy summer. A group of writers. One challenge: write a ghost story.
   _(tone: smug, a little slower)_

3. [PANEL] So they were stuck inside and said, let us write scary stories? Peak vibes.
   _(tone: excited, smiling)_

4. [PLOT] Mary Shelley began the story at eighteen. It was published in eighteen eighteen.
   _(tone: smug, a little slower)_

5. [PANEL] Published anonymously? Why would you hide a masterpiece?
   _(tone: puzzled)_

6. [PLOT] Plenty of authors then did that. The book still became a sensation.
   _(tone: smug, a little slower)_

7. [PANEL] My biggest project at eighteen was a playlist. I feel behind.
   _(tone: deflated, quiet)_

8. [PLOT] Rule of the day. A strange challenge can start a masterpiece.
   _(tone: smug, a little slower)_

9. [PANEL] Follow for more! Next up, an animated film that won an Oscar.
   _(tone: excited, smiling)_
