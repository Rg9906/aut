# Recording sheet: The Lord of the Rings is not a trilogy

File name: **f02-lotr.m4a** (or .wav / .mp3). Target length about 43 seconds. Mode: **dual**.

## How to record
- Phone voice memo, quiet room, phone about a hand-width from your mouth. No music or fan.
- One take, start to finish. If you flub a line, pause, say it again, keep going (I keep the matching words).
- Leave about one second of silence between numbered lines. Read the words exactly as written.
- Say it with energy. It should feel like a chat with a friend, a bit more hyped than normal.

- Two characters, both you: **PANEL** = the hyped one, **PLOT** = the calm, smug one. Change your tone a little for each.

## Read this

1. [PANEL] Quick! Is The Lord of the Rings a trilogy? Yes or no?
   _(tone: curious, thoughtful)_

2. [PLOT] Plot twist. Tolkien called it one novel. The trilogy was a publishing decision.
   _(tone: smug, a little slower)_

3. [PANEL] But I have three books on my shelf! Three covers! Three spines!
   _(tone: annoyed, louder)_

4. [PLOT] They were published in three volumes. The reasons were practical, mostly cost.
   _(tone: smug, a little slower)_

5. [PANEL] So the story is one giant book that got cut into three pieces?
   _(tone: puzzled)_

6. [PLOT] Yes. That is why the endings of volumes feel like pauses, not finales.
   _(tone: smug, a little slower)_

7. [PANEL] That actually explains why I always want to keep reading right away!
   _(tone: surprised, big energy)_

8. [PLOT] Rule of the day. A story can be bigger than its covers.
   _(tone: smug, a little slower)_

9. [PANEL] Follow for more! Next up, the horror classic written by a teenager.
   _(tone: excited, smiling)_
