# Recording sheet: Why One Piece sold over 500 million copies

File name: **f01-onepiece.m4a** (or .wav / .mp3). Target length about 45 seconds. Mode: **dual**.

## How to record
- Phone voice memo, quiet room, phone about a hand-width from your mouth. No music or fan.
- One take, start to finish. If you flub a line, pause, say it again, keep going (I keep the matching words).
- Leave about one second of silence between numbered lines. Read the words exactly as written.
- Say it with energy. It should feel like a chat with a friend, a bit more hyped than normal.

- Two characters, both you: **PANEL** = the hyped one, **PLOT** = the calm, smug one. Change your tone a little for each.

## Read this

1. [PANEL] One Piece has sold over five hundred million copies! What is the secret?
   _(tone: excited, smiling)_

2. [PLOT] No spoilers. Just structure. Three reasons this story keeps pulling people in.
   _(tone: smug, a little slower)_

3. [PANEL] That number is wild. Over five hundred million copies in circulation!
   _(tone: surprised, big energy)_

4. [PLOT] Reason one. Long term promises. The story plants mysteries that pay off hundreds of chapters later.
   _(tone: smug, a little slower)_

5. [PANEL] Reason two. The crew. Every character feels like a main character.
   _(tone: excited, smiling)_

6. [PLOT] Reason three. The world keeps growing. Every island feels like a brand new story.
   _(tone: smug, a little slower)_

7. [PANEL] So the secret is patience, friends, and a world you want to keep exploring?
   _(tone: curious, thoughtful)_

8. [PLOT] Rule of the day. A great series makes you want one more chapter. Always.
   _(tone: smug, a little slower)_

9. [PANEL] Follow for more! Next up, why a famous trilogy is not a trilogy.
   _(tone: excited, smiling)_
